# Accuracy audit — part-3/06-in-process-event-bus

Tier-2 chapter (correctness-critical: in-process pub/sub; claims read directly against Tailscale's production `util/eventbus`). Report-only; no edits applied to the content file.

## Critical finding (the assigned landmine: Tailscale eventbus internals)

**The chapter's read of `util/eventbus` is accurate, and it even self-corrects the subtle part.** The brief flagged the exact tension: doc.go documents *blocking* backpressure, but the current code gives **unbounded per-subscriber queues** with only the **central queue bounded (16)** (issue/PR #18020). The chapter gets this right in two places — the "Backpressure & the Pump" QuickCheck explanation and the "Tailscale's Real Bus" comment block both say: per-subscriber `DeliveredEvent` queues are now **unbounded** (cites #18020), the central queue is **bounded at 16**, and doc.go still documents the blocking model but marks it **"provisional and subject to change."** I confirmed all of this against the live `main` source. No defect on the landmine.

One internal inconsistency worth noting: the teaching bus's "Problem 4" section presents the bounded-queue `Full()`/`acceptCh()→nil` mechanism as the production design, then the later QuickCheck/Real-Bus sections correctly say the real per-subscriber queues are now *unbounded*. That's not wrong (the `acceptCh()`/multi-way-select machinery genuinely exists in subscribe.go; only the boundedness flipped), but a reader could think the bounded version is current. The chapter does flag it, so it's IMPRECISE-at-worst, not WRONG.
