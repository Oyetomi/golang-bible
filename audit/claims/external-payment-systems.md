**Worst finding:** #1 — the webhook HMAC is computed over the **raw body alone**, but the chapter's lead example provider (Stripe) signs **`timestamp + "." + rawBody`**. A reader who implements the chapter's `verifyWebhookSignature` verbatim against Stripe will reject every legitimate webhook (signature mismatch), and — more subtly — signing only the body leaves the replay-timestamp outside the MAC, so a forger could alter the timestamp the dedup layer trusts. The constant-time-comparison lesson is correct; the *signed payload construction* is the defect.
